# BigChange Academy Hub — fee adjustments and sample receipts (M5.13)

Manual review is deferred at the owner's request. All amounts are fictional and any recorded amount refers to an imaginary external payment; the Hub does not process payments.

## Included
- Edit the expected amount or Gregorian fee month on an existing fictional fee row.
- Reject duplicate learner/month obligations and reject lowering the expected fee below the amount already recorded.
- Preserve recorded amounts when adjusting the expected fee.
- Record browser-only history for fee adjustments and external-payment simulations; Reports distinguishes them.
- Generate a printable receipt-style sample after recording a fictional external payment. It is explicitly marked as a demo and not proof of payment.
- No payment processor, actual bank transaction, official receipt, notifications, or durable accounting records.

## Review checklist — not run
1. Finance: edit a sample fee month and amount; confirm the main ledger and Reports totals update.
2. Try assigning the same learner a month already used by another row; it should reject.
3. Try reducing a fee below its recorded amount; it should reject.
4. Record a sample payment; confirm a clearly marked demo receipt appears with amount and reference.
5. Use Print sample receipt and check only the receipt is printed; it must say it is not proof of payment.
6. Open Reports and confirm fee adjustments and sample payment records are described separately.
7. Refresh and confirm changes and history reset. Never enter real payments or family data.
